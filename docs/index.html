About Us

Our Story

Vet Restaurant is a proudly Rwandan dining establishment located in the heart of Kigali. We specialize in offering a delightful blend of traditional Rwandan dishes and international cuisines, carefully prepared with fresh, locally sourced ingredients.

Since our opening, we've become known for our warm hospitality, relaxing atmosphere, and consistently high-quality food that reflects Rwanda's vibrant culinary heritage.

Our Mission

To provide our guests with exceptional dining experiences that celebrate both Rwandan and international flavors, served with professionalism, warmth, and care.

Our Vision

To be one of Rwanda's most trusted and loved restaurants, known for quality, authenticity, and innovation in the culinary industry.

What We Offer

From traditional dishes such as Isombe, Brochettes, and Akabenz to international favorites like Grilled Chicken, Steak, and Lasagna — our chefs take pride in every plate served.

We also provide catering services for events, private dining experiences, and take-away orders.
